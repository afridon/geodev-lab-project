# My project brief

## The question
Which areas of Bridgwater, Somerset, are exposed to river, tidal, and surface-water flooding, and which buildings and roads are located within these areas?

## Required data
- Bridgwater boundary: https://osdatahub.os.uk/data/downloads/open/BoundaryLine
- Surface water: https://environment.data.gov.uk/explore/c853dc8c-9371-4a8f-99ea-a1daa023945e?download=true
- Buildings: Ordnance Survey - https://osdatahub.os.uk/data/downloads/open/OpenZoomstack
- Roads: Ordnance Survey - https://osdatahub.os.uk/data/downloads/open/OpenRoads
